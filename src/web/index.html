      How far is the drive?

      City coordinates come from Open-Meteo’s place search. OSRM calculates driving distance and travel time on mapped roads. Traffic and charging time can change the journey. Live weather and solar irradiance in the Grid workspace come from Open-Meteo’s Weather Forecast API.
